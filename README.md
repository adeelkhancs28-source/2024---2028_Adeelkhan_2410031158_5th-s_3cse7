📌 About the Job Simulation

The Deloitte Data Analytics Job Simulation was a self-paced, task-based virtual experience designed to simulate realistic data analytics and forensic technology work.

The simulation focused on applying data analysis concepts to a client-style business scenario, including:

- Understanding a business problem
- Identifying relevant data sources and data types
- Cleaning and structuring data
- Analyzing datasets
- Creating dashboards and visualizations
- Identifying trends, patterns, and anomalies
- Interpreting analytical findings
- Preparing actionable business recommendations
- Presenting findings in a professional, client-ready format

The simulation was completed through Forage and resulted in a Certificate of Completion issued by Deloitte through the platform.

🎯 Objectives

- Understand the role of data analytics in forensic technology.
- Identify relevant data sources for a business scenario.
- Clean, organize, and structure raw data for analysis.
- Analyze data to identify meaningful patterns and anomalies.
- Create dashboards and visualizations.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business insights.
- Develop professional client-style communication and presentation skills.
- Understand how data analytics can support forensic investigations, fraud detection, and risk advisory activities.
